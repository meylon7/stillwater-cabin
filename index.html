A note from the shore

We built it for the hour after dinner.

The lake goes flat around seven. The light drops out of the firs, the mist comes back across the water, and the whole valley stops making noise. That hour is the reason this cabin exists, and everything in it is arranged to keep you in it.

No television. One road in, and it ends here. We stock the kitchen, split the cedar, and leave you alone.

  • Sleeps 6

    Three bedrooms, two baths. Nobody draws the short straw and takes the sofa.

  • 180 ft of private shoreline

    The dock, the swimming rock and the canoe are yours. No shared access.

  • 40 min from the Cascades foothills

    Close enough for a morning trailhead, far enough that you hear nothing from the road.

  • 100% yours alone

    The whole cabin, every stay. We never split it and we never double book.
